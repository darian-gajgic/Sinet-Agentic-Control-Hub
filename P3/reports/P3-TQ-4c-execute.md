## 82. A detected rung is evidence on its own lane, and a failed one is a visible note, never silence and never a kill (P3-TQ-4c, 2026-10-02; S07.3, S07.5, S07.6, S07.7, S07.8 A16, S13.4; §70, §79, §80)

- **Attribution per origin lane (S07.3 first-upstream-failure; S07.8 A16).** `RunV1` keeps two lanes. The ANY lane records the earliest FAIL of either origin. The OWNER lane records FAILs of rungs the project captured (`Check.Origin != "detected"`). An owner rung is UNVERIFIABLE-HERE only past an earlier OWNER failure, attributed to the owner's first failure. A detected rung is UNVERIFIABLE-HERE past the earliest failure of ANY origin: a detected test on a tree the owner's build refused is undecidable, and it is evidence anyway, so it mints nothing when blocked. A platform guess therefore never silences an owner rung. `stepContracts` gets the OWNER lane's first failure, so no step contract is ever attributed to a detected id. Same-stage rungs all run, and the quarantine and runner-failure arms are unchanged.
- **A failed detected rung mints one NOTE at the decision (S07.7; S07.5).** `detectedNote` has severity note, the check's DECLARED category (never a literal), criterion `""` and anchor `check:<id>` (production: `check:detected:<slot>`). It is plain words: what the platform ran, "exit status N", that it decides nothing on its own, and that capturing the command makes it an authoritative check (the S13.7 Commands door), plus the re-bounded output tail. It is marked `fromCheck`. Citing nothing is what makes it a note: it never cites `check:<id>`, which remains the owner kill's admission alone (§80). Only FAIL mints. A blocked, unrunnable, quarantined or runner-failed detected rung mints nothing new.
- **Key and dedupe.** Key = `|check:<id>|<cat>` is round-stable (no status, no output). It cannot collide with the owner kill's `check:<slot>|check:<slot>|<cat>`, because `packChecksFor` emits ONE rung per slot, `<slot>` or `detected:<slot>`, never both. This is pinned by the 8×8 composition proof in `internal/shell`, and no dedupe code exists.
- **Survives round>1 suppression by the unforgeable mark (S07.6; §70 reasoning).** `validateFindings` exempts `fromCheck` notes from the new-note suppression, because a detected rung first failing at round 2 is a fact about this round's verification, not a drifting goalpost. A judge note of the identical shape is unmarked (the mark is unexported and invisible to JSON) and stays suppressed. That guard is pinned.
- **Downstream is unchanged and proven by test.** `ComputeVerdict` gives SHIP-with-notes. `reviewable` passes the note to `Review.RecordFindings` and on to the S13.4 drain as a numbered note. On a graduated round `VerifyLedgerItems` runs (items verified: the owner's own bar passed). There is no card and no integrity card, and `stop.observe` ignores notes. The `verify.v1` row, round record, `verdict.recorded` row and the round-2 judge's prior findings carry it. The receipt, `verifyTerminal` and `RevisionVerification` are byte-unchanged.
