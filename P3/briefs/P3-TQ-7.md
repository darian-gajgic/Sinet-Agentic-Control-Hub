# P3-TQ-7 — a failed captured check is a V1 kill: it mints a finding that cites the check, and the round cannot SHIP

**Grounded 2026-09-18.** Single-use brief for the executor and the evaluator. Truth is the spec (`Spec/drafts/S07-verification-quality.md` S07.1/S07.3/S07.5/S07.6/S07.7/S07.8/S07.11; `Spec/drafts/S13-deliverables-review-git-backup.md` S13.4/S13.7) plus the code on `main` at `c3ad07e` (every `file:line` below is that commit; the two inert surfaces this grounding adds to `v1.go` shift its later lines by +10). No prior brief was read as truth.

**The finding this packet closes** (surfaced by the P3-TQ-6 grounding; `P3/STATE.md` queue row P3-TQ-7): production `packChecks` binds NO step id and NO criterion to the captured `lint`/`build`/`test` checks (C13), and `RunV1` mints NO finding for a `CheckFailed` outcome (C1). So a captured `build` that FAILS is recorded on the `verify.v1` row and marshalled into the judge's evidence slice — and nowhere else. With a content judge that marks every criterion met, `ComputeVerdict` sees no blocker (C8), the round is SHIP, `VerifyLedgerItems` flips the work items to verified (C10) and the stage leg marks the task done (C11). `packChecks` itself declares each check's route as AC-BLOCKER — "rework round, then a requester decision card at cap" (C13) — and nothing realizes it. That is the P46 class S07.7 forbids ("a finding that died in a log"), live in production. `TestTQ7FailedOwnerCheckIsAKill` reproduces it on `main`: `no finding anchored check:build … []`.

**The coordinator's spec reading (binding for this packet; presented at the gate as a reading, not an amendment).** S07.1: "V0/V1 kill broken output before any paid call" — a failed captured V1 check is a KILL, so the round cannot SHIP. The S07.5 citation rule ("every blocker MUST cite the frozen criterion it violates (or an axis-2 rubric item); a finding that cites none can only be a note or a REOPEN-SPEC matter") governs the JUDGE's `[F1..Fn]`. A platform-raised V1 machine finding cites the CHECK that failed (`check:<id>`) under the check's declared finding category (S07.3: "a stage contract is incomplete unless it declares its finding categories and their escalation routes"; `packChecks` declares AC-BLOCKER), and `validateFindings` admits that criterion for V1-origin findings ONLY — the judge may never cite a check id as a criterion (goalposts stay fixed: the pack is the project's own frozen, verified-on-stamped bar, S07.3 rule 7). A REVISE round follows with the finding in the retry package; at cap the CAP-HIT card. Readings rejected: note-only (toothless, contradicts S07.1); bind-every-check-to-every-step (multiplies checks); first-upstream-FAIL-for-every-step (blunt).
