## 1. The evidence (verified this session, 2026-08-28)

### 1a. The A15-cap crash, end to end

Witnessed on the GF9 evidence world (`control.log`; times +02:00):

- **Eleven over-cap refusals** across two tasks, all the same class: `planner output: intake: invalid artifact: step S-1 approach is N characters (cap 1200)` with N = 1294, 1475, 1400, 1479, 1439, 1569, 1500, 1273 (t-33c01d7c2a613175) and 1249, 1246, 1434 (t-4b8ed0297f821433). Every N is within ~1.31× of the cap.
- Two surfacing shapes, one fate: an answer-driven advance dies as `stage: intake answer died mid-drive; crashing the run for the recovery ladder` (23:44:48, 00:03:59, 00:25:47); a fork's dispatch dies as `scheduler: dispatch … err="stage: intake advance: planner output: …"`. Both crash the run.
- The ladder then forks **with zero new information** — the fork re-drives the same Draft/Revise with the same prompt — so recovery is pure chance (the 23:45 fork happened to emit under cap; the 00:05/00:10 forks did not). Chain `t-33c01d7c….intake.g3 → .g7 → .g8` → **tombstoned 00:15:02** (⚙ `recovery.max_attempts` default 3, `internal/settings/index.go:100`). The tombstone-review card answered "retry" admitted `.g9` (00:20:36, the RW-14 door) → four more refusals → **tombstoned again 00:39:58**. The 00:03:59 crash is the redraft AFTER the approval card opened at 00:00:26 — the contest leg dead-ends exactly where E5-REWALK-2 must walk.

Root cause, in code (all verified):

1. **The contract omits the bounds.** `pairSchema` (`internal/stage/engines.go:372-397`) is the ONE emission contract Draft and Revise wrap. It states the A15 duty richly ("the material decisions you made … why the winner won") and **never states any length bound** — not 1200, not 400, not 8. The boundary (`Step.validateApproach`, `internal/intake/artifact.go:217-257`, called from `Plan.Validate`) enforces caps the planner was never told. A frontier model told "plain words … alternatives … why" and no budget writes ~1.1–1.3× over. GF8 built one half of a boundary.
2. **There is no bounded re-emission for a contract-invalid emission.** `pairSession` (`engines.go:433-469`) validates and returns `fmt.Errorf("planner output: %w", err)` straight up. The only bounded re-ask at the seam is `jsonRetryLimit = 1` (`engines.go:298`) — parse failures only. GF8's brief claim "an invalid emission errors exactly as any invalid emission does today" was TRUE and was the defect: "today" meant crash.
3. **The invalid-emission path IS the crash path.** `Surface.mapDriveErr` (`internal/stage/surface.go:137-155`) crashes any running run whose drive errored — correct doctrine for infrastructure death (§56), wrong diet: a deterministic contract refusal enters the ladder, which "can never fix a deterministic failure" (`internal/recovery/recovery.go:656`). The nine-refusal loop lived in the ladder's fork-redrive cycle, bounded per lineage at ⚙ 3, then re-armed by the tombstone card's retry.

### 1b. The confirm-loop, end to end

Task t-4b8ed0297f821433 (content family, low tier, force-proceeded interview). Card sequence: interview(1) → clarification(2) → decision.research(3) → clarification(4) → clarification(5) → clarification(6, open at shutdown) — **four clarification rounds**. DB forensics (asks table, snapshots + answers, read from a copy):

- Ask 2 marker: placeholder shop details must be replaced → requester supplies name/hours/mail. Ask 3: requester re-supplies the same facts as P47 supplied-facts ("all my own facts").
- Ask 4 marker-1 is a **statement, not a question**: "The shop name, opening hours, and contact email were supplied by the requester (…) and are shown as the shop's real details." Requester: "Yes — those are final, use them exactly as given." (Marker-2, the `.example` TLD catch, was a genuinely new point — the one honest marker in the run.)
- Ask 5 re-confirms both again → "Confirmed — final, build with exactly these."
- **Ask 6 is byte-identical to the answered ask 5** (both marker texts), issued 72 s later.

Root cause, in code:

1. **The mechanism is the post-draft marker loop**, not the S06.5 interview and not the ReviseInterview merge: `phaseSpine` cards ANY `pair.Spec.Clarifications` (`spine.go:188`, `pipeline.go:1206-1222`) — **no round bound** (coverage has `⚙ intake.coverage_autofix_rounds`, research has `ResearchBounced`, critique has `⚙ intake.critique_revise_rounds`; markers have nothing).
2. **The answered-marker record evaporates.** `applyClarificationAnswer` (`answer.go:308-330`) folds answers into a one-shot `ReviseReq{Reason: ReviseMarkers}` consumed by the next Revise (`pipeline.go:1111-1131`, then `PendingRevise = nil`). Nothing durable remains for the platform to detect a re-ask against, and later Draft/Revise inputs no longer carry the answers.
3. **The contract never says settled facts stay settled.** `pairSchema`'s only marker sentence is "unresolved consequential ambiguities become NEEDS-CLARIFICATION entries" — nothing forbids re-emitting a marker for an answered/supplied fact, and the witnessed planner uses markers as confirmations.

## 2. Requirements

Every requirement carries its S-ref and evidence ref. "The seam" = `EnginePlanner.pairSession`; "the pipeline" = `internal/intake`.

**R1 — the contract states the bounds (S06.6 [A15]; evidence 1a-1).** `pairSchema` states all four A15 caps in plain words, numerals included, **interpolated from the exported cap aliases** (`intake.ApproachMaxRunes`, `DecisionFieldMaxRunes`, `MaxStepDecisions`, `OrderingMaxRunes` — added inert at grounding) — never a second literal (the §65 D4 one-spelling rule). Reader's units: "characters" (the refusal's own word), never "runes". Required statements: approach at most 1200 characters (a few honest sentences — the decisions have their own fields); each decision/alternative/why field at most 400 characters; at most 8 decisions per step; ordering rationale at most 400 characters. Red: `TestGF12PairSchemaStatesTheA15Bounds`.

**R2 — the seam gets a bounded re-emission with the refusal fed back (S06.10 "bounded single revisions"; CONVENTIONS §60; evidence 1a-2).** In `pairSession`, a parse-valid emission that fails `pair.Validate()` is re-asked as a fresh session with the contract restated plus a platform-authored note carrying the refusal's exact specifics (e.g. "your previous emission was refused: step S-1 approach is 1294 characters (cap 1200) — restate it within the bound; shorten, never pad"). Bound: structural constant `emissionRetryLimit = 2` beside `jsonRetryLimit` (see §6; §60 blesses exactly this machinery — "the bounce recovers a malformed envelope"; this extends it to a malformed CONTRACT, same trust posture: platform validates, seam re-asks). Each re-ask logs Warn with run/stage/kind/attempt and the refusal — platform-authored fields only (S01.11). The refusal text itself is platform-authored (`validateApproach`), so it may ride the note and the log. Exhaustion returns the refusal error as today (R3 catches it). **Never a silent trim — model output is never repaired (§60); the platform re-asks or refuses, it never edits.**

**R3 — the pipeline lands an emission fault honestly: a served card, never a crash (S06.1 gates-wait; S06.7(a) requester-granted-round precedent; evidence 1a-3).**
- In `phaseInterview` (Draft) and `phaseSpine` (Revise), after the existing `handleEscalation` check: an error matching `errors.Is(err, ErrBadArtifact)` issues **`CardEmission` (`"decision.emission"`)** — a `DecisionBody` card, plain-words summary of what happened (which step/field, the refusal, how many rounds were spent), choices `ChoiceReplan` ("Try once more" — an explicit paid grant) and `ChoiceRethink` (cancel; the SPEC-DOUBT mapping). No new choice vocabulary. The run parks on the ask (`issueCard` does both).
- The fault is durable state (the `PendingEscalation` precedent): which op faulted (`draft`/`revise`), the refusal text, rounds spent. On `ChoiceReplan`: re-drive the SAME op with NOTHING lost — a draft fault re-enters `PhaseInterview` with `NeedsDraft` still true and all resolutions intact; a revise fault re-enters `PhaseSpine` with `PendingRevise` intact (it is only cleared after `acceptEmission` succeeds — keep that ordering). On `ChoiceRethink`: `PhaseCancelled` (the `applySpecDoubtAnswer` shape). `Answer` gains the `CardEmission` case.
- `mapDriveErr`/`Skeleton.crash`/the ladder are **untouched** — §60: the ladder stays the sole owner of crashed-advance recovery; this class simply stops crashing. Any other drive error keeps today's posture.
- The band: an emission fault cards even on a band task — the fault card is not intake ceremony (S06.4 bounds ceremony); the alternative is a crash, and "no blocking gate" never meant "die silently". Note this reading in the impl commit.
- Reds: `TestGF12OverCapDraftLandsOnACardNeverACrash`, `TestGF12EmissionCardRetryGrantsOneMoreRound`, `TestGF12OverCapReviseKeepsTheContestAndLandsHonestly`.

**R4 — the contract says settled facts stay settled (S06.5 resolution rules; evidence 1b-3).** `pairSchema` gains the settled-facts rule, stating: a fact the requester answered, supplied, or confirmed (the resolutions/supplied/clarification-answers blocks of the input) is **SETTLED** — never emit a NEEDS-CLARIFICATION entry that re-asks, **re-confirm**s, or restates one; markers are only for **new consequential ambiguities** nothing in the input resolves; markers are questions, never disclosures or confirmations. (The three bolded fragments are pinned by the red.) Red: `TestGF12PairSchemaSaysSettledFactsStaySettled`.

**R5 — clarification answers become durable record (S06.5 "a resolved slot must never re-ask"; evidence 1b-2).** Answered markers persist on `State` (marker text + the requester's answer + timestamp; a new field, the `Escalations` precedent — riding every state event) and are handed to every subsequent `DraftInput`/`ReviseInput` (new seam-input member; the fixture's fake planner ignores it harmlessly). The revise-input serialization (`reviseInputItem`) then carries them to the real planner, which is what R4's rule binds against.

**R6 — the settled-marker guard (S06.6: "each marker is either asked (S06.5) or converted to a listed assumption"; evidence 1b, ask 6 ≡ ask 5).** In `acceptEmission`: a marker in `next.Spec.Clarifications` whose whitespace-normalized text equals an already-answered marker's text is **settled from the record** — removed from the open markers with the requester's recorded answer landing on the SPEC (disposition per OQ-4; default: a listed assumption carrying question and answer), and a platform `RecordDecision` line disclosing exactly what was settled and from which answer. Never silent (§60's spirit; S06.7(a)'s "never disappears silently"). This is not model-output repair: `acceptEmission` already normalizes the SPEC's platform-guaranteed content (supplied facts, converted-slot assumptions) — the marker is not dropped, it is RESOLVED, because it was asked and answered (S06.6's first arm, satisfied from the record). Red: `TestGF12SettledMarkerIsNeverReAsked`.

**R7 — the marker loop is bounded (S06.6's second arm; S06.10 economics; evidence 1b-1).** Structural constant `clarificationRoundLimit = 2`: at most 2 clarification cards per intake. A genuinely new late ambiguity is therefore never swallowed — it is listed, visibly, on the card the requester answers anyway.
